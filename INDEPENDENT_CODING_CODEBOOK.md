# Independent semantic-coding codebook

Purpose: enable observer-independent coding without showing the authors' final admission labels or desired numerical contrasts.

For each relation field, use one of: `same`, `documented_near_match`, `changed`, `unresolved`, `not_applicable`. Do not inspect or compute the reported robustness ratio before completing the semantic fields.

Final admission options: `Strict`, `Qualified`, `Inadmissible`, `Unresolved`.

- Strict: same registered target/construct/domain/time/event universe/unit and matched or deliberately registered support.
- Qualified: only the construct is a documented near-match and the caveat can travel with the claim; all other required relations remain admissible.
- Inadmissible: a claim-defining target, event universe, domain, unit, or unregistered support relation changes outside the registered comparison.
- Unresolved: available source evidence is insufficient to determine a required relation.

Record a short evidence-grounded rationale. The packet is prepared for future independent coding; no inter-rater statistic is included because no independent coding exercise has been completed.
