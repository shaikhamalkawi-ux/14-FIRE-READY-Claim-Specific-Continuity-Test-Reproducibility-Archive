4. Scientific scope
The archive supports a claim-specific continuity test. Deterministic
verification is conditional on the registered semantic coding. Independent
semantic-coding reliability was not evaluated. This is not external validation.
SOCR is implementation/serialization, not a separate contribution. The archive
is not a generic ranking, scoring, MCDA, fuzzy, or decision framework.
